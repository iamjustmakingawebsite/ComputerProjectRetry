Introduction to Computers

Computers are electronic machines that accept data, process it, store information, and provide output.

Why Did Computers Evolve?

Computers evolved because people needed faster, more accurate, and more reliable ways to calculate, communicate, organize information, and automate repeated work.

Key Highlights

  • Early computers were large machines designed for calculations.
  • Transistors replaced vacuum tubes and made computers smaller.
  • Microchips helped create personal computers.
  • The internet connected computers globally.
  • Modern computers are used in phones, aircraft, cameras, games, and medicine.
